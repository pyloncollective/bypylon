02 / PILLARS & CAPABILITIES

HOW WE ENGAGE CLIENTS & AGENCIES

We operate as an integrated production ecosystem: taking campaigns from rough ideation through on-set execution and final cinema delivery.

Our creative arm develops bespoke director treatments, visual worldbuilding decks, scriptwriting, and brand campaign architectures built to win competitive agency pitches.

  • Director Pitch Treatments & Moodboards
  • Campaign Ideation & Script Development
  • Storyboard Architecture & Animatics
  • Brand Worldbuilding & Narrative Strategy

Full-tier commercial and narrative line production. We deploy seasoned Toronto union and indie crews, ARRI/RED cinema camera packages, anamorphic glass, and on-set supervision.

  • Full Line Production & Line Producer Bidding
  • Union & Indie Crew Manifests (DP, G&E, Sound)
  • Camera & Lighting Gear Packages (ARRI / Cooke)
  • Location Scouting, Permitting & Insurance

Finishing powerhouse partnered directly with Jackson McMurdo’s Muk Post. Complete DaVinci Wide Gamut CST color grading, offline/online editorial, custom film print emulations, and 5.1/stereo audio master QC.

  • DaVinci Wide Gamut CST Master Color Grading
  • Custom 35mm/16mm Film Stock Print Emulations
  • Sound Design, Foley & Broadcast Stereo/5.1 Mix
  • DCP Theatrical & ProRes 4444 XQ Archival Delivery
