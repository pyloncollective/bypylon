Who We Are 02

Pylon Pictures is a Toronto production company. Directors, cinematographers, designers, producers and finishers under one roof.

We build commercials, music videos, narrative shorts and branded films from first treatment to final master. One ensemble carries the idea from the pitch deck to the grade, so nothing gets lost in the handoff.

Eleven filmmakers, one studio. We keep crews nimble, decisions fast and the craft obsessive.
