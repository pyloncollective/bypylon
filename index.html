Who We Are 02

Pylon is a Toronto production collective. Directors, cinematographers, producers and finishers under one roof.

We build commercials, music videos and branded films from first treatment to final master. One team carries the idea from the pitch deck to the grade, so nothing gets lost in the handoff.

Eleven members, one studio. We keep crews small, decisions fast and the craft obsessive.
